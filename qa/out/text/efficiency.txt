Assumptions and maths

Your numbers, not ours. Every line is shown in full.

+ (120 emails × 5 days × 2 min ÷ 60) × 80% × 60% = 9.6 h

Total 114.4 h per week ÷ 37.5 h = 3.05 FTE
114.4 h × 46 weeks × €55 = €289,315 a year

Monthly volumes are converted to weekly by dividing by 4.33 (52 weeks ÷ 12 months). Email volumes use a 5-day week.

Hours are time freed up for people to spend on better work, not headcount cuts. Agents draft and people approve, so review time is already netted off in the “% saved” figures.
