Security and control

The same rules apply to every connection and every agent

Stays in your tenant

Data stays in your own Microsoft tenant, hosted in the EU region. Nothing is used to train public models.

Role-based access

Technicians see their site, Finance sees finance, directors see everything. Agents get the same limits.

Full audit log

Every read, draft, approval and change is recorded with who, what and when.

Approval before action

Nothing is sent, submitted, booked or paid by an agent without a person saying yes.
