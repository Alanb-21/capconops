## Commercial and site practice (from industry.md)
- Ireland: Construction Contracts Act 2013: payment claim notice, 21-day response; RCT.
  UK: Construction Act: application = default payment notice, pay less notice, final date for
  payment; CIS. Monthly applications against main contractor cut-off dates.
- Retentions 3–5%, half at practical completion, balance after the defects period.
- Variations: instructed (often verbally on site) → priced → submitted → agreed.
- BCAR ancillary certificates at completion. BS EN 12056-3 design; BS 8490 siphonic maintenance.
- Siphonic: electrofusion/butt-welded HDPE, weld logs, water/air tests witnessed.
- Tickets: Safe Pass (IE), CSCS (UK), IPAF 3a/3b, PASMA, manual handling, working at height,
  site inductions. Pharma/data centre sites: RAMS approval, permits to work, inductions.
