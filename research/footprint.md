- **Hiring a QS "design to final delivery"** suggests that commercial capacity (applications, variations, final accounts) is a current stretch point.
- **Technician hiring for UK and Ireland** brings ticket and competency tracking across two card schemes (Safe Pass/QQI vs CSCS).
- **Design + prefab + install + certify + maintain** is an end-to-end lifecycle, which suits a single job record running from design, prefab spools, install and test to the BCAR ancillary cert and BS 8490 maintenance visits.
- **Sites like Dexcom and data-centre or pharma work** mean heavy RAMS, permit and induction overhead, and coordination with roofers on outlet installs.
- **Best Place to Work / people-first** messaging means demo copy around reducing admin burden for technicians and site staff will land well.
