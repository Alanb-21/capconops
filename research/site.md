## Company snapshot
- Legal name: Capcon Engineering Ltd (CRO 491226, per SoloCheck). A sister entity "Capcon Engineering Global Ltd" (CRO 716131) also exists. Source: solocheck.ie
- HQ and prefab facility: Unit G2-G4, Maynooth Business Campus, Straffan Rd, Maynooth, Co. Kildare W23 V2P5. All pipework is "pre-fabricated off-site in their prefabrication facility in Maynooth" (mmcireland.ie member profile).
- **Do not confuse with** "Capcon" at Newhall Business Park, Naas (capcon.ie, a logistics/roll-cage company) or "CAPCON Group" on LinkedIn, which appears to be a different group.
- Positioning: "Rainwater drainage specialists": design, pre-fabrication, installation, certification and maintenance of bespoke siphonic and gravity rainwater drainage systems.
- Scale claims: "more than 250 rainwater drainage systems in Ireland, the UK and Europe since 2009" (Enterprise Ireland directory snippet); "100s of projects across Ireland and the UK" (MMC Ireland).
- History (capconeng.com/capcon-15-years-transforming-rainwater/): roots around 2008/09 recession; siphonic drainage became the differentiator. Larger complex projects 2010-2016. In early 2016 a full restructure dropped all side work to focus solely on rainwater management, R&D, and new methods for large-scale siphonic and gravity systems. Another source gives operations starting in 2011. "Celebrating 10 Years in Business" post exists.
- Headcount: LinkedIn lists 11-50 employees. A post mentions "over 40 Capcon employees" at a Volunteer Day.
- Awards: shortlisted for Specialist Contractor of the Year at the Irish Building and Design Awards 2019.
- Markets: Ireland, UK, Europe, plus Singapore and Malaysia (design consultancy).
- Social: facebook.com/Capconeng, youtube.com/@capconengineering, LinkedIn.

## Services
- Siphonic rainwater drainage (primary and secondary/overflow systems)
- Gravity rainwater drainage (including polyester powder-coated aluminium gravity systems)
- Hybrid siphonic + gravity schemes
- Design consultancy: "expert PI-backed rainwater drainage design service" during the planning phase, working with engineers and architects. BIM Level 2 accredited design team; 3D design integrated into the federated BIM model. Can coordinate with BIM or CAD teams.
- Supply and install. "Technicians are experienced in the installation of all leading siphonic and gravity drainage brands including Geberit Pluvia" (capconeng.com/supply-install/).
- Off-site prefabrication (Maynooth)
- Testing and commissioning, air-testing, BCAR certification
- Maintenance / service. There is a "Service Sales Manager" role.
- Rainwater harvesting
- External siphonic drainage (Malaysia data centre)
- Siphonic Drainage CPD for consulting engineers and architects. "An Introduction to Siphonic Drainage for Engineers" is listed as an Engineers Ireland registered training provider course, delivered by Design Lead Stephen Morris. Also on Cademy.
- Tender enquiries page: capconeng.com/tender-enquiries/

## Demo-relevant observations (inference, not sourced)
- Their work runs as a project pipeline: tender, then design (BIM), then prefab in Maynooth, then site install, then air test/commission, then BCAR cert, then maintenance. That flow maps onto a project and ops tracker.
- KPIs they publish themselves: metres of pipe (siphonic vs gravity), roof area m², number of systems (primary/secondary), number of outlets, l/s capacity, project duration.
- They are growing internationally (Singapore, Malaysia) and have a service/maintenance revenue line.
